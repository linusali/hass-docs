== What this is

Our villas came with built-in, proprietary home automation - closed systems you
can't extend, inspect, or get parts for without depending on a single vendor. One
villa in Phase 4 has successfully replaced its *TIS Control* system with
*Home Assistant*, keeping every existing switch and sensor in service.

The key insight that makes this nearly free: **the motion sensors and TIS light
switches already installed in our villas communicate over Zigbee** - an open
standard. They don't need to be replaced or rewired; they just need a new
coordinator to talk to. The only hardware you buy is a small computer and a
Zigbee coordinator (see the bill of materials in the
link:phases/phase-4/guide-technical.adoc[Technical Guide]).

This repository is the central place to gather what each part of the community
learns: guides, per-phase notes, and a growing library of real automations you
can copy into your own setup.

== How it all fits together

image::architecture-simple.svg[Simple architecture overview,800,,align="center"]

The short version: a small computer (a Raspberry Pi) running Home Assistant becomes
the new "brain" of the villa, replacing the built-in control panel. The Zigbee
switches and motion sensors already in your walls are re-paired to it - nothing is
bought twice, nothing is thrown away - and the villa's own VRF air-conditioning is
controlled over the local network. None of this requires an internet connection to
work day-to-day; remote access from outside your home is optional and covered in
the link:phases/phase-4/guide-technical.adoc#_remote_access[technical guide].

== Getting help

This is a DIY, neighbour-to-neighbour effort - there is no official support and
community management is not involved.

* Start with the two guides and your phase's page.
* Ask the neighbour(s) who've already done the conversion before experimenting
  alone - the WhatsApp group is the right place.
* If you find an error in these docs or improve on the setup, contribute it back
  (a pull request, or pass it to whoever maintains this repository) so the next
  neighbour benefits.

== Roadmap

* **RS485 integration** - bringing the villas' RS485 bus into Home Assistant to
  unlock the additional built-in motion sensors and the water- and
  power-usage meters. Tracked in the
  link:phases/phase-4/guide-technical.adoc#_roadmap[technical guide's roadmap section].
* **Phase 1-3 documentation** - waiting on a resident of each phase to document
  what their villas shipped with.
* **Growing the automation library** - every villa that converts is a source of
  new examples.
