== In one paragraph

https://www.home-assistant.io[*Home Assistant*] is a free, open-source program
that runs on a small computer in your home and acts as the central brain for
everything "smart" in it - lights, switches, sensors, air-conditioning,
media players, cameras, and thousands of other device types. It is developed in
the open by one of the largest open-source communities in the world, works
entirely on your local network (your data stays in your house), and is
controlled through a web page or a polished phone app.

== Why people choose it

* **It's free.** The software costs nothing, forever. You only buy hardware.
* **It's local and private.** Unlike cloud-based smart home products, it keeps
  working when the internet is down, and your home's data isn't sent to anyone's
  servers.
* **It speaks everything.** Over https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/[3,000
  integrations] connect it to devices and services from virtually every
  manufacturer - including, via this community's work, the TIS hardware in our
  villas.
* **It's yours.** No vendor can discontinue it, paywall a feature you rely on, or
  end support for your hardware.

== The words you'll keep seeing

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Term | What it means

| *Integration*
| A connector that teaches Home Assistant to talk to a particular brand or type
  of device - like a driver. Example: the `hass-tis-vrf-ac` integration connects
  it to our villas' air-conditioning.

| *Entity*
| One controllable or observable thing: a light, a motion sensor, a temperature
  reading, an AC unit. Everything in Home Assistant is an entity.

| *Automation*
| A rule: _when_ something happens (motion detected), _if_ conditions are met
  (it's after sunset), _then_ do something (turn on the hallway light). You
  build these with a visual editor - no programming needed.

| *Script*
| A reusable sequence of actions you can trigger from anywhere - like a "movie
  night" button that dims lights and sets the AC.

| *Dashboard*
| The screen you see in the app or browser - fully customizable panels of
  buttons, graphs, and status displays for your home.

| *Add-on*
| An extra program running alongside Home Assistant on the same computer,
  installed with one click - e.g. Zigbee2MQTT (Zigbee radio management) or
  Tailscale (remote access).

| *Zigbee*
| An open wireless standard that battery-friendly smart devices use to talk.
  The switches and motion sensors already in our villas speak it - which is why
  we can reuse them.

| *HAOS (Home Assistant OS)*
| The ready-made operating system image you flash onto the Raspberry Pi - it
  boots straight into Home Assistant with no Linux knowledge required.
|===

== What it looks like day to day

You open the app (or a browser) and see your home: which lights are on, the
temperature in each room, whether the AC is running. You tap to control
anything. Behind the scenes, your automations quietly do their jobs - lights
that follow motion at night, AC that switches off when nobody is home, a
notification if a water sensor gets wet. Guests and family don't need to learn
anything: the wall switches still work exactly as wall switches always have.
