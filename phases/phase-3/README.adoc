= Phase 3: To Be Documented
:toc!:

Nobody has documented Phase 3's built-in home automation hardware yet - this
page is waiting for a Phase 3 resident to fill it in.

== What we need from a Phase 3 neighbour

If you live in Phase 3, you can kick this off with even partial answers:

* What brand/system is the built-in control panel (photos of the panel and its
  labels help enormously)?
* What do the light switches look like, and is there a brand/model on them
  (often inside the frame or on a sticker in the wall box - *do not open wall
  boxes yourself; a photo of the front is already useful*)?
* Are there motion sensors, and what do they look like?
* How is the air-conditioning controlled - its own wall thermostat, or through
  the main panel?

Post findings in the WhatsApp group or contribute them here directly. Once we
know what the hardware is, the community can work out whether the same
zero-cost re-pairing approach used in link:../phase-4/README.adoc[Phase 4] applies.
